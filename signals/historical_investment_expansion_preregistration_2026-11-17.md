# Historical expansion and point-in-time universe preregistration — 2026-11-17

## Purpose
Attack the two largest remaining risks in the 2023–2025 investment result: small historical sample and hindsight/survivorship in the 2026-curated ticker-policy exposure map.

No newly reconstructed return outcomes may be used to alter Quant weights, policy definitions, selection threshold, 90-day primary horizon, or the accepted quarter-end + 20-day information lag.

## Historical extension
2022 and 2021 belong to the 117th Congress. The repository does not currently contain an outcome-blind frozen 117th bill-policy manifest comparable to the 118th manifest used for 2023–2024.

Primary extension therefore starts with deterministic activity-text evidence only. Bill-anchor evidence is excluded rather than retroactively mapped after return outcomes are available. These scores are labeled activity_text_only_historical_sensitivity and are not silently pooled with the exact-replay 2023–2025 series. Before any return test, compare their score/rank behavior against an activity-text-only replay of 2023–2025.

If a reproducible 117th official bill corpus and conservative title-owned mapping can later be frozen without consulting returns, a separate bill-anchor-enhanced reconstruction may be run.

## Point-in-time company exposure universe
The 2026 curated ticker-policy map remains a benchmark only. A stronger historical exposure test must require evidence that the company was economically connected to the policy at the signal date.

Primary rules: company/ticker identity is frozen independently of future returns; company must be public/price-observable on the signal date; company must have contemporaneous LDA client/activity evidence in the policy during the signal quarter (or a deterministic corporate-parent alias established without return data); no company may be added because it subsequently performed well; delisted/failed companies should be retained whenever a reliable historical security identity and price series can be recovered; ambiguous identities remain unmapped.

## Investment test carried forward unchanged
Signal: quarter-over-quarter Quant economic-pressure change. Selection: top quartile within signal quarter. Availability: first trading day on/after quarter-end + 20 calendar days. Primary horizon: 90 calendar days. Primary benchmark: SPY. Robustness: sector ETF, top-minus-bottom, leave-one-quarter/policy-out, random-policy placebo. Portfolio: equal weight at security level, quarter-level inference.

## Acceptance interpretation
The 2023–2025 result remains the frozen benchmark. Historical extension is confirmatory only if direction and economically meaningful excess returns survive without changing these rules. Failure is reported as failure; no optimization against historical returns is permitted.
