# Institutional Target Expansion Audit — 2026-11-10

Goal: increase independent corroboration without weakening evidence-independence standards.

## Method

Candidate policy/institution relationships were evaluated independently in each 2025 quarter.
Generic House, Senate, White House, and EOP targets were excluded. New rules were admitted only
when minimum quarterly institution precision was at least 15% and lift remained strong in every
quarter.

## Important conclusion

Institutional targets alone cannot responsibly reach the provisional 15% promotion gate with the
current data. Relaxing the specificity floor would inflate corroboration with broad, weakly
diagnostic agencies.

The remaining gap should come from a genuinely different evidence family rather than looser
institution mappings.

Recommended next research family: independently reported committee/legislative-contact structure,
but only after proving it is not derivative of the same bill reference. If that independence test
fails, use a different external/behavioral source rather than count it as corroboration.
